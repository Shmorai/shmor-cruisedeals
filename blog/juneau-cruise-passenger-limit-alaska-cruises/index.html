Juneau's cruise passenger limit: what it means for your Alaska cruise

Juneau is one of the busiest ports on an Alaska itinerary, and the city has agreed daily limits with the cruise industry. Here is what the official agreement says, what it doesn't say, and how to plan an Alaska cruise around it.

By the CruiseDeals editorial team · 25 November 2026 · 2 min read

What is the Juneau cruise passenger limit?

In June 2024 the City and Borough of Juneau signed a Memorandum of Agreement on port capacity with cruise lines. The city's announcement describes a limit of 16,000 lower berths per day, and 12,000 on Saturdays. The parties also committed to meet every year to review visitor numbers, adjust the schedule and discuss community goals. News coverage at the time reported that the limits would apply from the 2026 season; the city's announcement page doesn't state a start date, so check the city's current information for the sailing you are booking.

What does "lower berths" mean?

A lower berth is the standard measure of a ship's passenger capacity, counting two guests per cabin. The cap is therefore based on the capacity of the ships in port, not the number of people who actually walk down the gangway. That is why it doesn't tell you exactly how many people will be in town on a given day, and why you should treat it as a guide rather than a promise of quiet streets.

Does it affect your Alaska cruise booking?

For most passengers, the practical effect is on scheduling: the cruise lines and the city coordinate which ships call on which days. Your ship's Juneau day, and how long it stays, is set in the itinerary, so look at it before you pay. Confirm the details with the cruise line, as itineraries can change. Tips for a busy port day in Juneau

  • Book excursions early. Popular tours (such as glacier and whale-watching trips) can fill, so reserve through your cruise line or a licensed local operator before you sail.
  • Plan the first and last hour. Check the all-aboard time and leave margin when returning to the ship.
  • Have a Plan B. Weather can change plans in Alaska, so keep an indoor option in mind.
  • Compare itineraries. Some sailings visit other Alaska ports instead of, or as well as, Juneau. Compare routes on our cruise deals page.

Frequently asked questions

How many cruise passengers can visit Juneau a day?
The agreement signed by the City and Borough of Juneau and cruise lines sets a limit of 16,000 lower berths per day, and 12,000 lower berths on Saturdays, according to the city's announcement.
Is the Juneau limit the same as the number of people on shore?
Not exactly. It is counted in lower berths, which is a measure of ship capacity rather than the number of people who go ashore.
Will the Juneau limit change my cruise itinerary?
Your itinerary is set by the cruise line, so check your booking. The agreement itself says the parties will meet annually to review and adjust the schedule, so details can change over time.
